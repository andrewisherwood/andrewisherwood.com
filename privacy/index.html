Privacy notice

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This notice explains what happens to the personal details you send me through this website.

Who I am

This site is run by Andrew Isherwood, a web designer and developer based in Wimbledon, London. I'm responsible for the personal details you send me. You can reach me at build@andrewisherwood.com.

What I collect

If you use the contact form, I collect what you type into it: your name, email address, company (if you give one), the type of project, your budget and timeline, and your message.

If you email me directly, I receive your email address and whatever you include in the email.

I don't collect anything else. This site doesn't use cookies, analytics or tracking, and it doesn't load fonts or scripts from other companies.

Why I use it

I use your details only to reply to your enquiry and, if we decide to work together, to provide a quote and get the project started. My lawful basis is that you've asked me to get in touch, so it's needed to take steps before we enter into a contract, and it's in our legitimate interests for me to answer you.

I will never sell your details or add you to a mailing list.

Who else sees it

Contact form submissions are processed and stored by Netlify, the company that hosts this website. Netlify is based in the United States and handles the data under its own data processing terms. Replies are sent from my business email account.
